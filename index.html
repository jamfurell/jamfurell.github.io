My Process

When something breaks, the first move isn't a fix — it's a frame.

Scoping impact, verifying what's actually observed versus what's assumed, and understanding where in the system the signal is coming from. That foundation is what makes everything after it — the investigation, the communication, the resolution — move with purpose and clarity.
What follows is how I work.

01 Intake

Scope before solving

The first question isn't "what broke" - it's "what's actually being reported, and how wide the impact is." I seperate confirmed behavior from assumed behavior before touching anything

02 Investigation

Test symptoms, not assumptions

I break the probelm into testable components - API behavior, log patterns, data state, etc - and validate each one before forming a conclusion. Speculation gets replaced with evidence.

03 Communication

Honest checkpoints

Throughout an investigation, I keep status clear - what's confirmed, what's still being tested, and what comes next. It helps all stakeholders be aligned and adjust priorities as new information comes in, so decisions and next steps are clear.

04 Escalation

Escalate with context

When engineering involvement is needed, I come with structured reproduction details, narrowed scope and documented key findings - not just a ticket that says "something is broken".

05 Resolution

Close the loop

Resolution isn't done when the fix goes in. I follow through to confirm the outcome, set realisitc expectations throughout and make sure the person on the other end actually understands what happened and next steps.
